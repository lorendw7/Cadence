**Status:** Accepted delivery decision. No mobile feature, test result or release is established by this document.
**Date:** 2026-11-15
**Decider:** The maintainer explicitly selected an Android self-use edition first, with advertising deferred.

## Context

The maintainer has an Android phone and is willing to use Cadence daily. No external trial users are available. They want eventual recovery of cash and development time, but neither purchases nor advertising income have been validated. The app still keeps tasks in memory. The current lesson and unfinished checks remain in [LEARNING.md](../LEARNING.md).

[ADR-014](014-desktop-focus-and-mobile-ads.md) placed the Windows focus package and purchase study before Android. That order delays feedback from the device the maintainer can actually use. A safe local planner can be useful before a timer, music player, desktop scene or sales channel exists.

## Decision

1. Make the ad-free Android self-use edition the first delivery target. Keep Windows as the development host and a useful shared-code test surface. A complete Windows release is not a prerequisite for Android.
2. Continue the current small lesson, then build shared local saving and recovery. A fictional-data Android launch and layout check may happen early. Real personal records require confirmed saves, local/temporary isolation, restart recovery and tested backup/restore on the phone.
3. Deliver in small usable slices: safe tasks first, then direct personal plans, Today and a selected-day Week view, then bounded repeats and occurrence corrections. Recheck backup compatibility as records grow. Expose only implemented controls. A safe task-only slice is not the complete planner.
4. Keep quick capture title-first. Notes and deadlines are optional. A deadline does not reserve time; a work plan does not complete a task. Keep readable dates, touch controls, keyboard handling, Back behavior, text scaling and Japanese/English/Chinese together with each slice.
5. Run the maintainer's two-week self-use check after the planning flow and native recovery checks pass. No recruited cohort is required to continue development. Record friction and failures privately. Self-use is personal and technical evidence, not independent demand, retention or willingness to pay.
6. Separate private installation from a public mobile release. A local Android build does not establish Play approval. Before public distribution, complete packaging, updates, support, privacy and any applicable store testing requirements. Self-use cannot waive those rules.
7. Keep ads out of development's first delivery milestone and the first self-use build. A later pilot requires a useful mobile release, an ad-free usage baseline, a bounded budget and its own decision. Keep the existing optional review-screen placement limits. No rewarded unlocks, forced interstitials, paid ad removal or required ad viewing.
8. After reliable planning and the first self-use check, add a bounded Android task-linked countdown or simple Pomodoro flow: start, pause/resume, end, manual break and small local session records. Agree on the learner-owned interruption and saving rules first. No automatic cycles, background-execution guarantee or closed-app alarms are promised. Keep task completion independent from timer completion.
9. Defer desktop compact mode, pins, larger review surfaces, music integration, scene polish, Steam preparation and desktop purchase validation. [DESKTOP_V1.md](../DESKTOP_V1.md) remains the bounded later Windows candidate. Reuse the accepted shared timer rules and prepared assets when selected; no fal top-up or new paid generation is authorized.
10. Keep one Flutter codebase and the existing GitHub Pages concept site. School authorization, application preparation and integration are explicitly postponed. Imports, accounts, cloud sync, billing, Wallpaper Engine and additional platforms remain separate later work. Desktop, Web and the site stay ad-free. MIT and the pending repository-visibility decision are unchanged.

## Delivery and evidence

[PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md#feature-priorities) owns priorities. [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md#working-order) owns gates and effort reviews. [CURRICULUM.md](../CURRICULUM.md) keeps learning-critical state, data, async and first Flutter implementations with the learner. No new package, architecture layer or future lesson implementation is added by this decision.

Review progress every 20 working hours and replace estimates with actual effort after saving/recovery and the first native slice. Keep past hours in lifetime cost recovery. Do not fund the next milestone from hypothetical ad income. A useful self-use app may still have no commercial market.

## Precedence

This supersedes ADR-014's desktop-before-Android order and the current-priority status of its desktop first offer. It also supersedes earlier ADR-012/013 wording that requires a complete Windows product before phone validation. Their historical decisions remain readable. Local data rules, general planning, deferred school access, independent branding, mobile-only ad limits and the later desktop purchase hypothesis remain.

This decision changes the plan, not implementation status. It does not approve spending, outreach, store enrollment, publishing, remote visibility changes or a final price.
